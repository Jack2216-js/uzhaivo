# Uzhaivo mobile updates

## Owner job discovery and management
- Owners can browse all active available jobs in a Jobs tab.
- Owners can still see and manage their own jobs in My Jobs.
- Owner job cards show the current remaining Need count.

## Worker contact privacy
- Remove the worker Calls tab, call history, call buttons, and direct call actions.
- Keep owner contact details hidden while proof review is pending and reveal contact text only after approval.

## Owner verification
- Owners can submit proof from the profile Owner Verification section.
- Admin-controlled verification state determines whether the owner receives an Admin verified badge.
- Admin-verified owner status is shown on the owner profile and on job cards to build worker trust.

## Application approval capacity
- Approving a worker application decrements the job Need count.
- When Need reaches zero, the job is automatically removed.
- Worker application status is updated and the worker is notified after approval or rejection.

## Applied Jobs cleanup
- Remove an applied job from the worker's Applied Jobs list when the owner deletes it.
- Remove an applied job when its remaining Need reaches zero and the job is completed.
